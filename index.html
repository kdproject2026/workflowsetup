⚡ Workflow 1 — Initial Outreach

Day 1 • Entry Point

Purpose: Fire the first SMS, wait 24h, then either route to Cold Lead Stage (if reply) or hand off to Workflow 2 (if silent).

Trigger
Contact Tag Added: land-lead-new
Exit Tag (Reply)
hot-lead-replied
Exit Tag (No Reply)
no-reply-day1
Send Window
9:00 AM – 7:00 PM (contact timezone)
Workflow Steps
1Trigger Trigger
Contact Tag Added → land-lead-new
2If/Else — Suppression Check Condition
Has Tag opted-out OR do-not-contact? → Exit Workflow
3Send SMS — Initial Outreach SMS
Hey {{contact.first_name}}, we have caught interest in your lot at {{contact.address1}} are you ready to get rid of it? Reply STOP to opt out.
4Wait — 24 Hours Wait
Wait duration: 24 hours (or "Wait until next day 10:00 AM" for cleaner sends)
5If/Else — Did They Reply? Condition
Check: Contact has tag hot-lead-replied (set by separate Inbound SMS trigger — see Bonus #1)
✅ YES — Replied
Add tag: cold-lead-stage
Remove tag: land-lead-new
Send internal notification to sales rep
→ Exit Workflow
❌ NO — Silent
Add tag: no-reply-day1
Remove tag: land-lead-new
→ Triggers Workflow 2 automatically
💡 Pro Tip: Set up a separate "Inbound SMS Received" workflow that auto-applies hot-lead-replied the moment any reply comes in. This way every workflow's If/Else can simply check for that tag.

🔁 Workflow 2 — Week 1 Follow-Up Drip

Day 2 – Day 7 • 7 Follow-Ups

Purpose: Send Follow-Up 1 through Follow-Up 7 one day apart. After every send, check for reply. If yes → Cold Lead Stage. If no reply by end of FU7 → Workflow 3.

Trigger
Contact Tag Added: no-reply-day1
Exit Tag (Reply)
hot-lead-replied → cold-lead-stage
Exit Tag (No Reply)
no-reply-week1
Total Duration
~6 days (FU1 fires Day 2, FU7 fires Day 7)
Workflow Steps (Repeat Pattern × 7)
1Trigger Trigger
Contact Tag Added → no-reply-day1
2Wait — Until Next Day 10:00 AM Wait
Ensures FU1 fires Day 2 morning, not random hour
3If/Else — Already Replied? Condition
Has tag hot-lead-replied? → YES: jump to Exit-Reply branch (step 18). NO: continue.
4Send SMS — Follow-Up 1 SMS
Hi {{contact.first_name}}, just circling back on your lot at {{contact.address1}}. Did our message reach you? Happy to share a cash offer if you're open to it.
5Wait 24h → If/Else Reply Check Wait + If
Wait 1 day → check hot-lead-replied → if YES exit, if NO continue
6Send SMS — Follow-Up 2 SMS
Hey {{contact.first_name}}, we buy land directly — no agents, no closing costs, we cover everything. Still interested in your lot at {{contact.address1}} if you'd like a no-obligation number.
7Wait 24h → If/Else Reply Check Wait + If
8Send SMS — Follow-Up 3 SMS
Hi {{contact.first_name}}, a lot of owners we work with hold land they no longer use but still pay taxes on it. If that sounds like {{contact.address1}}, we'd love to make it easy for you.
9Wait 24h → If/Else Reply Check Wait + If
10Send SMS — Follow-Up 4 SMS
Hey {{contact.first_name}}, we recently closed on a few parcels in your area. Quick, clean, no surprises. Open to seeing what we can offer for your lot at {{contact.address1}}?
11Wait 24h → If/Else Reply Check Wait + If
12Send SMS — Follow-Up 5 SMS
Hi {{contact.first_name}}, simple yes/no — are you open to selling {{contact.address1}} this year if the price was right?
13Wait 24h → If/Else Reply Check Wait + If
14Send SMS — Follow-Up 6 SMS
{{contact.first_name}}, would you rather we stop reaching out, or keep you on our list in case we can help later? Just want to respect your time.
15Wait 24h → If/Else Reply Check Wait + If
16Send SMS — Follow-Up 7 (Closer) SMS
{{contact.first_name}}, this'll be our last note for now. If selling {{contact.address1}} ever crosses your mind, text us anytime — we'll be ready. Thanks!
17Wait 24h — Final Reply Window Wait
18Final If/Else — Replied at Any Point? Condition
✅ YES — Replied
Add tag: cold-lead-stage
Remove tag: no-reply-day1
Notify rep
→ Exit Workflow
❌ NO — Still Silent
Add tag: no-reply-week1
Remove tag: no-reply-day1
→ Triggers Workflow 3
💡 Pro Tip: Use GHL's "Goal Event" feature — set the goal as "Tag hot-lead-replied Added". Any contact who replies mid-drip jumps straight to the exit branch automatically, no manual If/Else needed at every step.
⏸ WAIT 1 MONTH (between Workflow 2 and Workflow 3)

🚀 Bonus Workflow Automations (Highly Recommended)

Stand-alone supporting workflows that make the core 4 bulletproof.

Bonus 1 — Inbound Reply Detector (Global)

Always-On

Purpose: The single source of truth for "did they reply?" Auto-tags any inbound SMS so all 4 workflows can branch cleanly.

Trigger
Customer Replied (Inbound Message → SMS)
Filter
Contact has any of: land-lead-new, no-reply-day1, no-reply-week1, no-reply-month1
Steps
1Trigger: Customer Replied → SMS Trigger
2If/Else: Message contains "STOP" / "UNSUBSCRIBE" / "REMOVE"? Condition
YES → Add opted-out + do-not-contact, send confirmation: "You've been removed. Reply START to resubscribe." → Exit
NO → continue
3Add Tag Tag
Add: hot-lead-replied
4Add Tag Tag
Add: cold-lead-stage
5Move Opportunity → "Engaged / Manual Outreach" stage Pipeline
6Internal Notification Notify
Email/SMS to sales rep: "🔥 {{contact.first_name}} just replied about {{contact.address1}}. Call now."
7Create Task Task
Title: "Call {{contact.first_name}} — replied to land outreach" • Due: in 2 hours

Bonus 2 — Opt-Out / Compliance Workflow

Compliance Critical

Purpose: Instantly suppress any contact who replies STOP. TCPA-required.

Trigger
Inbound SMS contains: STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, REMOVE, QUIT, CANCEL, END
Steps
1Add Tags: opted-out, do-not-contact Tag
2Remove Tags: All workflow stage tags Tag
Remove: land-lead-new, no-reply-day1, no-reply-week1, no-reply-month1
3Send Confirmation SMS SMS
You've been unsubscribed and won't receive further messages. Reply START to opt back in.
4Disable Marketing Channel DND
Set contact DND status → SMS: ON

Bonus 3 — Cold Lead Stage Nurture (Manual Outreach Support)

Sales Enablement

Purpose: When a lead enters Cold Lead Stage, give your rep a structured cadence of reminders.

Trigger
Contact Tag Added: cold-lead-stage
Steps
1Create Task — "Call within 2 hours" Task
2Wait 2 days Wait
3If/Else — Opportunity moved to "Negotiating" stage? Condition
YES → Exit. NO → Create Task: "Second call attempt"
4Wait 3 days → Create Task: "Send personalized offer email" Task
5Wait 7 days → If still no movement, notify manager Notify

Bonus 4 — Hot Keyword Detector (Buying Signals)

Sales Acceleration

Purpose: Catch high-intent replies like "how much", "offer", "yes", "interested" and escalate immediately.

Trigger
Inbound SMS contains: "how much", "offer", "yes", "interested", "price", "cash", "sell"
Steps
1Add Tag hot-buyer-signal Tag
2Move Opportunity → "Hot Lead" pipeline stage Pipeline
3Send urgent rep notification (SMS + Email + Push) Notify
"🚨 HOT BUYER SIGNAL — {{contact.first_name}} at {{contact.address1}} just said: {{message.body}}"
4Create Task — "URGENT: Call now" with 30-min due time Task

Bonus 5 — Wrong Number / Not Owner Handler

Data Hygiene

Purpose: Catch "wrong number" or "not the owner" replies, tag them, and stop all future sends.

Trigger
Inbound SMS contains: "wrong number", "not me", "don't own", "sold it", "no longer own"
Steps
1Add Tags: bad-data, do-not-contact Tag
2Remove all active workflow tags Tag
3Send polite acknowledgement SMS
Thanks for letting us know — we'll update our records and stop reaching out. Apologies for the bother.
4Notify data team for skip-trace cleanup Notify

Bonus 6 — Birthday / Holiday Soft Touch (Year-Round Nurture)

Long-Term Brand

Purpose: Keep your brand warm with non-salesy touches during the long quiet gaps between Workflows 3 and 4.

Trigger
Recurring → Specific dates (e.g., Thanksgiving, New Year)
Filter
Has tag no-reply-month1 AND does NOT have opted-out
Steps
1Send Holiday SMS SMS
Happy holidays from our team, {{contact.first_name}}! Wishing you a great year ahead. — [Your Company]
💡 Why this works: Soft brand touches mid-cycle dramatically increase reply rates on the Workflow 4 1-year touch — recipient already feels familiar with you.

Bonus 7 — Lead Scoring & Pipeline Auto-Mover

Reporting

Purpose: Auto-update opportunity stages based on tags so your pipeline view is always accurate without manual dragging.

Rules
When tag land-lead-new added → Pipeline stage: "New Lead" Pipeline
When tag no-reply-day1 added → Pipeline stage: "Following Up" Pipeline
When tag hot-lead-replied added → Pipeline stage: "Engaged" + score +25 Pipeline
When tag hot-buyer-signal added → Pipeline stage: "Hot Lead" + score +50 Pipeline
When tag dead-lead-archive added → Pipeline stage: "Archived" Pipeline
Build Order: Tags → Pipeline Stages → Bonus 2 (Opt-Out) → Bonus 1 (Reply Detector) → Workflow 1 → Workflow 2 → Workflow 3 → Workflow 4 → Bonus 3–7

Compliance: TCPA, state-level wholesaling rules, and quiet-hours (8am–9pm contact local time) apply. Always test with internal numbers before going live.
